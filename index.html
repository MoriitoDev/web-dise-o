Our story

One shelf, forty volumes and far too much coffee

NyanComics started in 2019 in a shared flat in Valencia, when two friends got tired of hunting down out-of-print volumes across a dozen second-hand sites. They stacked their own collection on a single shelf, photographed it with a phone and sold it in one weekend.

Seven years later that shelf has become a warehouse with more than 12,000 titles, but the rule is still the same: we only sell the manga we would lend to a friend. Every release is read, argued about and, more often than not, kept on our own shelves.
